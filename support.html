How to play

  • Tap anywhere to fire an arcane bolt toward that point.
  • Hold, then release when the Nova meter is full to cast an Arcane Nova that blasts every nearby enemy and skull.
  • Each enemy that reaches you breaks one ward. Lose them all and the level is over.
  • Banishing enemies quickly builds a combo multiplier, up to 3×.
  • Clear a level without losing a ward for 3 stars. Leftover wards are worth 250 points each.

Frequently asked questions

My bolts pass right through ghosts. Is that a bug?

No. From level 2, ghosts phase out for a moment. Wait until they become solid again, then fire.

Leaderboards or achievements aren't showing up.

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in Settings → Game Center, and that you're online. Achievement progress earned offline is saved on your device and sent the next time Game Center is available.

How do I earn the Starlight achievement?

Earn 3 stars on all 10 levels (30 stars). Replay any level from level select to improve your stars.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Level progress and stars are stored on your device, so deleting the app resets them. Your Game Center scores and achievements stay with your Game Center account.

Does Moonward have ads or in-app purchases?

No. Moonward is a one-time purchase with no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS / iPadOS 16 or later. The game plays in landscape.
