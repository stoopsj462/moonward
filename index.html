Hold the hilltop

The moon is up and the dead are restless. You are the last wizard on the ridge. Tap to hurl arcane bolts, chain banishes into combos, and unleash the Arcane Nova when they close in. Ten nights, each darker than the last, end at the throne of the Lich King.

Tap to cast

One-touch controls built for quick sessions. Tap anywhere to fire. Hold and release for a screen-clearing Nova.

10 haunted levels

From Moonrise Meadow to Starless Ridge, with fog, ghoul hordes and two boss battles against the Lich.

Combos & stars

Chain banishes for up to a 3× multiplier. Keep your wards intact to earn all three stars.

Game Center

Three leaderboards and eleven achievements, from First Light to Lichbane and Starlight.

Know your foes

GhostsWeave toward you, and later phase out so bolts pass right through.
GhoulsTough shamblers that crawl in from both sides.
WraithsFlash red in the sky, then dive straight at you.
BansheesHover high and hurl screaming skulls. Shoot them down.
The LichSummons the dead, fires skull volleys, and teleports when wounded.
