Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Moonward ("the app") is made by Jason Stoops. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services are included.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, best scores and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to me. Deleting the app removes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app submits your scores and achievement progress to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and in your achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple, and that information is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. I do not receive your Game Center account details. You can play without Game Center, and you can manage your Game Center profile and visibility in Settings → Game Center.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
